# Air Law — Quick Reference

*Fast recall only. Confirm current rules, local AIP procedures, and airspace status before flight.*

## VFR airspace at a glance

| Class | VFR access | VFR separation (headline) |
|---|---|---|
| A | Not permitted | — |
| B | Clearance required | Separation from all aircraft |
| C | Clearance/radio required | Separated from IFR; traffic information on VFR |
| D | Clearance/radio required | Traffic information; VFR not separated from VFR |
| E | VFR generally without ATC clearance | VFR not separated |
| F/G | Uncontrolled/advisory as designated | No ATC separation for VFR |

*Class services and VFR minima depend on the applicable rules and published airspace. Verify the chart/AIP.*

## Collision avoidance

```text
Head-on → both alter course right
Converging → give way to traffic on your right
Overtaking → keep clear; pass well clear
```

Never rely on right-of-way alone to prevent collision. Maintain lookout and take safe action.

## Before flight

- Check current chart, AIP, NOTAM, weather, aircraft documents, pilot privileges, and required equipment.
- AIP: GEN (general), ENR (en-route), AD (aerodromes).
- Separate the concepts: clearance = permission; instruction = required action; traffic information ≠ separation.
- Identify VFR/IFR, airspace class, altitude band, and local procedures before answering minima or entry questions.
