# Aircraft General Knowledge — Quick Reference

*Systems, limits, and emergency actions vary by aircraft; the POH/AFM and placards govern.*

## Structure and controls

| Structure | Load path |
|---|---|
| Truss/grid | Framework carries load; skin is covering |
| Monocoque | Skin carries most/all structural load |
| Semi-monocoque | Stressed skin + frames/stringers share load |
| Sandwich | Two face layers + light core |

Primary axes: longitudinal → roll/ailerons; lateral → pitch/elevator; vertical → yaw/rudder.

## Piston engine

```text
Four-stroke Otto: INDUCTION → COMPRESSION → POWER → EXHAUST
```

- Magnetos provide ignition independent of aircraft electrical power.
- Carburettor heat reduces icing risk, usually with an RPM/power reduction; use POH procedures.
- Engine oil lubricates, cools, and carries fine contaminants; a clogged filter bypass may preserve flow without filtration.
- Fuel: confirm grade, quantity, and freedom from contamination before flight.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Pressure/sensing source |
|---|---|
| Airspeed indicator | Pitot + static (dynamic pressure difference) |
| Altimeter | Static pressure |
| VSI | Static pressure change over time (lags) |
| Magnetic compass | Earth’s magnetic field; affected by aircraft deviation and motion errors |

- Airspeed arcs and \(V\)-speeds are aircraft-specific; respect placards and limitations.
- Gyros resist changes in orientation (rigidity); precession causes apparent movement under applied force.
- Check instrument failures against other indications and the POH; do not assume one faulty reading is correct.
